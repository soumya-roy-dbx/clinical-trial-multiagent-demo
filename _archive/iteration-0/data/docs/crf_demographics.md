# CRF — Demographics (MOCK)

## Purpose
Collect baseline demographic information for each enrolled subject.

## Fields
- `subject_id` — site-assigned unique identifier (S####).
- `age` — integer years at consent (50–85).
- `sex` — F / M.
- `region` — site region (Eastland / Northland / Westland).
- `arm` — randomized treatment arm (Treatment / Control).
- `biomarker_status` — Positive / Negative at screening.

## Notes
Demographics are collected once at the screening visit and locked at
randomization.
