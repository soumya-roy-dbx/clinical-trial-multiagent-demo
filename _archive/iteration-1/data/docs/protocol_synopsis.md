# Protocol Synopsis — Demo Study (MOCK)

## Study Title
A Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of Investigational
Compound X in adults with a target condition. **All content is mocked for demo purposes.**

## Objectives
- **Primary:** Evaluate the efficacy of Compound X vs. placebo on the primary
  efficacy endpoint at Week 52.
- **Secondary:** Characterize safety and tolerability; assess effect on
  biomarker-positive sub-population.

## Design
Randomized 1:1 to Treatment or Control, stratified by region (Eastland,
Northland, Westland) and baseline biomarker status.

## Population
Adults aged 50–85 with confirmed diagnosis. Target enrollment N=100 for the demo
dataset.
