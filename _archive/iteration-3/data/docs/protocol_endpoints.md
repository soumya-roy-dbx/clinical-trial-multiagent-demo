# Endpoints — Demo Study (MOCK)

## Primary Endpoint
Change from baseline at Week 52 in the primary efficacy measure, analyzed in the
intent-to-treat (ITT) population.

## Secondary Endpoints
- Proportion of subjects achieving a clinically meaningful response at Week 52.
- Change from baseline at Week 52 in the biomarker-positive sub-population.
- Time to first adverse event of interest.

## Safety Endpoints
- Incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs).
- Incidence of serious adverse events (SAEs).
- **Event A** and **Event B** are protocol-defined adverse events of interest.
