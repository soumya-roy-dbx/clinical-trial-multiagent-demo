# Inclusion / Exclusion Criteria — Demo Study (MOCK)

## Inclusion Criteria
1. Adults aged 50–85 at the time of consent.
2. Confirmed diagnosis of the target condition per protocol-defined criteria.
3. Able to provide written informed consent.
4. For the biomarker-positive sub-study: documented positive biomarker status at
   screening.

## Exclusion Criteria
1. Known hypersensitivity to Compound X or any excipient.
2. Participation in another interventional trial within 30 days of screening.
3. Pregnancy or breastfeeding.
4. Any condition that, in the investigator's opinion, would interfere with
   study participation or interpretation of results.
